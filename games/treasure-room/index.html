Treasure Room

One small room, six ideas: an agent, state-action-reward, return, policy, value and the MDP. Teaches that the AI only ever knows what the room tells it.

Play Watch How it works
The setup
A five-by-five room with a robot, a treasure worth +10, a pit worth -10 and a few walls. Every step costs 1, even a bump into a wall.
The rule
You drive the robot first. Then the AI takes over, and all it is ever told is where it stands and the reward it just got. Each level switches on one more idea.
Watch for
On level 1, the AI never sees the room itself, only its position and a reward. On level 2, every step lands in the log as a state, an action and a reward.
The lesson

Six small ideas, one room: the AI only ever knows what the room tells it.
