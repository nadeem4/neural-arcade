Neural Arcade How it works Gen 001

Neural Racer

The setup
Ten cars get random brains. Nobody teaches them how to drive.
The rule
After each round, only the cars that drove furthest have children.
Watch for
How many rounds until one car drives two full laps.

How it works What you are watching, step by step, with pictures. 5 min read.
Population How many cars try in each round. More cars means more random tries, so a good driver usually turns up sooner, but each round takes more work to run. Few enough to follow every car. Usually learns within a handful of rounds.

Generation 1: every brain is random, so most cars crash or stall. Watch for one that turns by luck.

Its brain

This brain is driving the orange car. Left: the six numbers it senses, its five feelers on the track plus its speed. Middle: 8 hidden neurons that mix them. Right: the two things it controls, steering and throttle. Each line is a weight: solid pushes the next neuron up, dashed pushes it down, brighter means more signal right now. The weights never change while it drives; only breeding changes them.
