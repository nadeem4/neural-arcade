        Race done

        A taxi learns to cross the real Chicago Loop, and you write its reward. The taxi does what you paid for, not what you meant. Teaches reward design.

        The setup
        A taxi on a map of the Chicago Loop. It starts on Michigan Avenue beside Millennium Park and has to reach State and Van Buren. Buildings and water block it. Parks and footpaths do not.
        The rule
        It starts by trying trips at random and keeps the ones that earned the most pay. You write the rate card: the fare, the clock, and a fine for each way of cheating.
        Watch for
        With only the clock on, it drives along the footpaths. Fine that, and it runs red lights. Fine those, and it goes the wrong way. Then fix it: a legal trip that is still fast. The five steps on the rate card walk you through it. Cars honk and people jump aside when the taxi cheats, but they are not in the reward, so it does not care.
        The lesson

        The taxi does what you paid for, not what you meant.
