      Your goal

      A taxi learns to cross the real Chicago Loop, and you write its reward. It does what you pay it for, not what you meant. Teaches reward design.

      The setup
      A taxi on a map of the Chicago Loop. It starts at the pickup and has to reach the drop-off. Buildings and water block it. Parks and footpaths do not.
      The rule
      It knows nothing at first. It tries trips and keeps whatever earned the most reward. You set the reward: the fare, the clock, and a fine for each way of cheating.
      Watch for
      With only the clock on, it drives across the park. Fine that, and it runs red lights. Fine those, and it goes the wrong way. Then fix it: a legal trip that is still fast.
      The lesson

      The reward is the only thing the taxi wants: it does what you pay it for, not what you meant.
