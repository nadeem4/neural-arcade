Reward City

A taxi learns to cross the real Chicago Loop, and you write its pay slip. It does what you pay it for, not what you meant. Teaches reward design.

Play How it works
The setup
A taxi on a map of the Chicago Loop. It starts at the pickup and has to reach the drop-off. Buildings and water block it. Parks and footpaths do not.
The rule
It knows nothing at first. It tries trips and keeps whatever earned the most on the pay slip. You set the slip: the fare, the clock, and a fine for each way of cheating.
Watch for
With only the clock on, it drives across the park. Fine that, and it runs red lights. Fine those, and it goes the wrong way. Then fix it: a legal trip that is still fast.
