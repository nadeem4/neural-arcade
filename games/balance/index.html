Balance

A cart must keep a pole upright. You, a PID controller with three dials, a PID whose dials evolution tunes and a tiny evolving network all try, and you can shove it with Push and Wind. Teaches feedback control, PID, evolution and disturbances.

The setup
A cart on a rail with a pole hinged on top. The pole falls unless the cart moves under it.
The rule
Whoever is in control reads the lean and pushes the cart under it: you with the arrow keys, a PID controller with three dials, a PID whose dials evolution tunes, or a tiny network that learns by evolution.
Watch for
P alone wobbles, D calms it, and Wind leaves a steady error that I removes. Then Tuned PID: evolution finds I on its own. Then the AI going from instant falls to balancing.
AI is balancing

Kicks the cart sideways, right then left on each press, so you can see how whoever is in control recovers.

Who is in control

You use the arrow keys or A and D, or the buttons on a touch screen. PID uses the three dials below, which you set. Tuned PID is the same PID, but evolution sets the dials. AI has no formula: it uses the best brain evolution has found so far.

PID dials

Copies the dials evolution has found right now into PID, where you can drag them yourself. Nothing else changes.

Training speed

The AI and the dials' tuner both train unseen in the background, in every mode. The stage replays the latest generation's best. Paused keeps both as they are.

2 generations a second

Throws away the AI's brain and the tuned dials, and starts both evolutions again from scratch.

Its brain

Its formula

Four numbers in (cart position, cart speed, pole angle, pole spin), 6 hidden neurons, 1 out: 37 weights in all. The output is how hard to push, left or right.

A person wrote this formula: push in proportion to the lean, to the lean added up over time, and to the spin. Evolution only chose the three numbers in front.

What it senses, and how hard it pushes right now.

The three dials turned into a rule, with the numbers it is using now.

Generations

One bar per generation: seconds the best brain of that generation stayed up, with random pushes thrown at it. In Tuned PID the bars are the best dials of each generation, scored over shoves and over wind. The dashed line is the 20 s limit.

How long has the best brain stayed up?

The lesson

Staying upright is constant correction: measure the error, push back. A tiny brain can find that rule on its own.
