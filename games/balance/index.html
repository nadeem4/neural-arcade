Balance

A cart must keep a pole upright. You, a PID controller with three dials and a tiny evolving network all try, and you can shove it with Push and Wind. Teaches feedback control, PID, evolution and disturbances.

Play Watch How it works
The setup
A cart on a rail with a pole hinged on top. The pole falls unless the cart moves under it.
The rule
Whoever is in control reads the lean and pushes the cart under it: you with the arrow keys, a PID controller with three dials, or a tiny network that learns by evolution.
Watch for
P alone wobbles, D calms it, and Wind leaves a steady error that I removes. Then the AI going from instant falls to balancing.
AI is balancing

Kicks the cart sideways, right then left on each press, so you can see how whoever is in control recovers.

A steady sideways force on the cart. Turning it on never resets anything, and the AI never practised with it.

Who is in control

You use the arrow keys or A and D, or the buttons on a touch screen. PID uses the three dials below. AI uses the best brain evolution has found so far.

Training speed

The AI trains unseen in the background, in every mode. The stage replays the latest generation's best brain. Paused keeps the brain as it is.

2 generations a second

Throws the AI's brain away and starts evolution again from random brains.

Its brain

Four numbers in (cart position, cart speed, pole angle, pole spin), 6 hidden neurons, 1 out: 37 weights in all. The output is how hard to push, left or right.

What it senses, and how hard it pushes right now.

Generations

One bar per generation: seconds the best brain of that generation stayed up, with random pushes thrown at it. The dashed line is the 20 s limit.

How long has the best brain stayed up?

The lesson

Staying upright is constant correction: measure the error, push back. A tiny brain can find that rule on its own.
