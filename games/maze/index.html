Treasure Maze

An explorer learns a small grid by trial and error, and you set how much far-off reward counts. Teaches reward, value, the discount and exploring.

Play Watch How it works
The setup
An explorer on a six-by-six grid. One tile holds a big treasure worth +10, another a small one worth +3, and some tiles are pits worth -10. Every step costs a little.
The rule
It knows nothing at first. After each move it updates a table of how good that move was, given the best it expects next. You set the discount, how much far-off reward counts, and the exploring, how often it tries a random move.
Watch for
On Near or far, a low discount sends it to the small treasure and a high one to the big treasure; keep exploring around 0.5, because with almost none (0.05 to 0.2) it settles on the small treasure even at 0.9. On Cliff edge, the more it explores, the more often it falls in the pit.
Light is value, arrows are its best move

Every tile starts at zero, so the first tries are random wandering. Watch the light spread back from the treasure.
