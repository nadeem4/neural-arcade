Part 3 A chain of decisions

  1. 5 StateThe state is what the agent knows about where it is. The best action depends on the state, and every action changes the state.
  2. 6 ReturnThe return is every reward still to come, added up. The discount makes rewards further away count a little less.
  3. 7 PolicyA policy is one action for every state: an arrow on every tile. A better policy earns more return.
  4. 8 ValueA state's value is the return you expect from there if you follow the policy. Store it on the tile and you never recount.
  5. 9 When moves slip: the MDPAn action now has odds, so a value is an average over what might happen. States, actions, rewards and odds together are called an MDP.

Part 4 Planning with the rulebook

  1. 10 One step ahead: the Bellman equationA tile is worth its reward plus the discounted worth of the tile it leads to. One step ahead is enough. Coming soon

  3. 12 Policy iterationScore the current plan, turn each arrow to its best neighbour, and repeat until no arrow changes. Coming soon

Part 5 Learning without the rulebook

  1. 13 Monte CarloPlay whole episodes and, for each tile you passed, average the returns you actually got from there. Coming soon
  2. 14 TD learningAfter every step, nudge this tile's value toward the reward plus the next tile's value. Coming soon
  3. 15 The value of a move: QGive every move on every tile its own value, called Q. To act, take the move with the biggest Q. Coming soon
  4. 16 Q-learningUpdate each move toward the reward plus the best move available next, whatever the robot does next. Coming soon
  5. Also in Treasure MazeThe explorer learns the best move for each tile by trying them.
  6. 17 SARSA: the safe pathUpdate each move toward the move the robot really takes next. It learns the path that is best for a learner that still slips. Coming soon
